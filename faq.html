Do you conduct training on our campus?

Yes. Our trainers come to your institution with industry-grade mannequins and equipment, so there's no travel or coordination burden on your side. We adapt to your academic calendar, shift patterns and available spaces.

Which programmes and certifications do you offer?

Three tracks: STARS Certified courses (First Aid, First Responder, BLS & BCLS, PALS, Newborn Care and more), JeevaRaksha-associated courses (BECLS, ENLS), and American Heart Association courses (BLS, ACLS).

How long do the courses run?

It varies by programme — from a focused 6-hour session (e.g. First-Aid Advanced, BLS, BECLS) up to 2–3 day intensives (e.g. ACLS, PALS, Emergency Nurse Life Support). We'll confirm exact duration when we build your plan.

How long are the certificates valid?

STARS Certified and JeevaRaksha-associated certificates are valid for 5 years. American Heart Association certificates are valid for 2 years.

Who are these programmes for?

Nursing and medical college students, doctors, nurses and paramedics, hospital and corporate teams, schools, and community groups. Courses are mapped to INC, IMC and AHA expectations and to each semester's learning stage.

How do we book training for our institution?

Send us the programme, participant group and preferred dates using the enquiry form, or reach us on WhatsApp, call or email. Our team will get back to you to finalise the schedule.
