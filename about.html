Who we are

Training that turns knowledge into decisive action.

STARS Training Academy is a professional training team committed to helping people respond effectively to emergencies. Every programme combines evidence-based instruction, practical demonstrations and realistic simulation.

From nursing and medical colleges to hospitals, corporate teams, schools and community groups, we bring life-saving knowledge directly to the people who need it.
